How It Works
The Rules of Life
  • Survival: A lit cell with 2 or 3 neighbors stays warm and lit.
  • Birth: An empty cell with 3 neighbors sparks to life.
  • Fading: With fewer than 2 or more than 3 neighbors, a cell dims out.
Interaction & World
  • Plant Seeds: Click or drag across the grid anytime to toggle or draw living cells.
  • Endless Torus: Edges wrap around seamlessly so gliders travel forever.
